M E R C Y M E D C O M PA N Y
OVERVIEW
Mission: To improve the quality of life of our patients through competent,
compassionate and comprehensive medical care. Patient care that is provided
by locally trained Curtis professionals.

Vision: To revolutionize the way healthcare is delivered in Kyrgyzstan
through a holistic approach of body and spirit.

Company Values:
Compassion, holistic care, integrity, trust, sustainable.

LOGO DESIGN
OVERVIEW
The logo consist of the emblem, which is an abbreviation letters for
Mercymed Medical center and the name.
Each stage of the emblem is done with different colors that represent
progression towards healing as well as pointing to the center meaning holistic
approach of the company towards health.
The name in the logo is done in gray and red colors representing
professionalism in medical care. The curved lines speak of care done
with compassion.
